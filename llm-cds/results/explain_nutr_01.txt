QUESTION  Our emergency nutrition survey found an acute malnutrition rate of 12% with no aggravating factors. Which food and nutrition interventions do the surveillance benchmarks call for?
ANSWER    Based on the nutrition surveillance benchmarks, an acute malnutrition rate of 10%-14% with aggravating factors requires emergency food aid (general food ration), blanket supplementary feeding, and therapeutic feeding of severely malnourished individuals [1]. However, for a rate of 10-14% with no aggravating factors, the guidelines do not explicitly list specific interventions, only stating that for rates <10% with no aggravating factors, no emergency food and nutrition intervention is required [1].
REFERENCE For an acute malnutrition rate of 10–14% (or 5–9% with aggravating factors): no general rations, targeted supplementary feeding, and therapeutic feeding of severely malnourished individuals.
